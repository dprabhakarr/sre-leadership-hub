Reliability is a Leadership Discipline

Reliability is not just an operations function. It is an engineering leadership discipline that sits at the intersection of technology, business strategy, and organizational design.

Modern engineering organizations need to balance competing demands: delivering reliable systems while moving at speed, scaling infrastructure while managing costs, maintaining security while enabling innovation, and building for the future while operating the present.
