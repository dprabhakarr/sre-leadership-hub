Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The SRE Leadership Hub respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit this website.

1. Information We Collect

The SRE Leadership Hub may collect limited information automatically when you visit the website, including information such as browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages and general usage information.

If you voluntarily contact us or subscribe to a newsletter, information such as your name and email address may be provided directly by you.

2. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic, visitor engagement and how users interact with the site's content. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage information.

Analytics information is used to improve the website, understand which content is useful to visitors and improve the overall user experience.

3. Cookies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, functionality and, if advertising is enabled in the future, advertising-related purposes.

Cookies are small files stored on your device that can help websites recognize a browser and understand how a website is being used.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

The SRE Leadership Hub may participate in Google AdSense or other advertising services in the future.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements and may use information associated with a user's visit to this website or other websites for advertising purposes, subject to applicable policies and user settings.

Users may manage personalized advertising preferences through Google's Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console, Google AdSense and external content or communication services.

These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Newsletter and Communications

If you voluntarily subscribe to communications from The SRE Leadership Hub, your email address may be used to send requested newsletters, updates or other communications.

You may unsubscribe from marketing or newsletter communications at any time using the available unsubscribe mechanism.

7. External Links

The website may contain links to external websites. The SRE Leadership Hub is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of external websites.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a professional and general audience and is not directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, analytics, advertising services or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on this website, please use the Contact page.
